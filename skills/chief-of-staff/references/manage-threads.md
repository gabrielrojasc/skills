# Manage threads

1. Settle each thread that is finished: its PRs merged, a newer thread replaced
   it, or it delivered a one-off answer. Before you settle a replaced thread,
   list what it found that its replacement lacks, and offer to send those
   points over.
2. Sort the rest into tables with chat links:
   - Needs me now, with my call.
   - Stuck on my decision, oldest first, with the date it started waiting.
   - My PRs that need upkeep, such as approvals, conflicts, or a stale base.
   - Snoozed, with each wake time.
3. Say which group to clear first, and why.
